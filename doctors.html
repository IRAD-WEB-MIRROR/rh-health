Doctors' reception times

The consultative polyclinic of the hospital is in the Diagnostic Center (Building F), 1st and 2nd floors; children in Building C (children's entrance). Specialists receive patients by referral (form 057/u) of the district polyclinic or of a district hospital: the consultation is then free. Without a referral the consultation is paid by the price list.

Registry: Building F, ground floor, windows 1-3, Mon-Fri 7:30-15:00, Sat 8:00-12:00, tel. +996-39-2-26-10-30. Coupons for the day are given from 7:30; registration for another day by telephone from 13:00. Bring your passport or ID card, the referral, and earlier test results.
