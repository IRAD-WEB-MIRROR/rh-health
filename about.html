About the hospital

The Talas Regional Hospital Medical Complex (Talas Ayymdyk Oorukanasy) is the main referral and teaching hospital of the Irad Special Economic Zone. It opened in 1954 as the regional clinical hospital of the Talas valley and was rebuilt and re-equipped in 1995-2002. See History.

Bedsabout 650 (703 in the departments listed on this site, the rest in day and observation wards)
Campus15 ha, Hospital Boulevard, about 0.9 km south-west of the central square
Staffabout 2,100, of them 390 doctors and 860 nurses and midwives
Patientsabout 31,000 inpatients and 140,000 outpatient consultations a year
Teachingabout 400 students, residents and nursing students of Talas State University each year
UnderMinistry of Health of the ISEZ

The Talas Blood Services Center (gate 2) is on the campus and supplies the hospital blood bank. The Ministry of Health is opposite, at 6 Hospital Boulevard.
